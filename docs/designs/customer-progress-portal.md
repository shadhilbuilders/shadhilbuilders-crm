## Problem Statement
Homebuyers with Shadhil Builders have no way to see construction progress on their
unit. Today a buyer emails the site manager and waits for a reply; there is no
self-service view, no photos, no status. The buyer's actual question is one:
"where is my house at, show me."

## Demand Evidence
- The demand signal the user named: customers email the site manager asking about
  unit progress. That is a real, observed request, not a hypothetical.
- Frequency and volume are not yet measured (see Open Questions). The direction is
  confirmed; the volume is not.

## Status Quo
Nothing happens. The user verified there is no existing workflow and no workaround
in place. This means any self-service loop is an improvement, and there is no
legacy behavior to migrate or preserve.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
The buyer of one specific unit. One-time customer for the duration of construction
(roughly 18-24 months), then gone. The wedge is still the photo timeline; the
umbrella is the full customer portal the user chose (Approach C) because the client
wants the complete surface.

## Premises (user confirmed all four)
1. Web app, mobile-first, PWA-installable. Not a native app. The planned Expo
   `apps/mobile` stays for staff use, not customers.
2. Customers get their own logins in the existing better-auth system, with a new
   Customer role. RLS scopes the customer to only the unit(s) on their own Booking.
   Customers never enter the org-scoped `[orgSlug]` routes.
3. Read-mostly split. Staff upload progress photos and phase updates in the CRM;
   customers view. No customer-created tickets, comments or payments in v1, even
   though the shell is built to absorb them later.
4. Sequencing: photo timeline ships first inside the portal shell. A customer with
   an empty docs tab but a live photo timeline still got value.

## Approaches Considered
### A) Photo timeline only
Site manager uploads site photos per unit from their phone; customer sees a photo
timeline with phase labels, web push on new upload. Smallest diff, ships days.
Rejected by the user in favor of C; its upload loop becomes Slice 1 of C.

### B) Weekly automated status link
Weekly email/link showing phase status from CRM data, no photos. Zero staff work,
ships fastest. Rejected: no photos means no emotional payoff; "phase 4 complete"
is not what a buyer wants to see.

### C) Full customer portal (CHOSEN)
Progress timeline + booking docs + payment views + messages under one customer
login. Most professional, one auth surface, absorbs A and B as slices.

## Site-staff model (amended 2026-10-26, user-approved "amend it")

Site progress upload is done by site engineers and site managers, NOT by
MANAGER-role users and NOT via the sales CRM pages.

- New Role enum value: `ENGINEER`. Held by every site person. Its RLS rights
  are only: read + upload `ProgressPhoto` rows for units in projects where
  their team is linked (ProjectTeam) and they hold a TeamMember row. No leads,
  no chats, no bookings, no revenue anywhere. Site staff never hold MANAGER;
  MANAGER stays the sales leadership role it is today.
- Supervision is a team permission, not a role: `TeamMember.photoLead: boolean`
  (default false). photoLead=true adds exactly two powers: correct/delete
  another engineer's photo (T7 correction work, supervisor-only) and
  invite/remove engineers on the project (T4 invite action). The site manager
  is the member with photoLead=true; one or more engineers may exist per site.
- Where photoLead is set in the UI (both screens already exist):
  1. Role: Admin > Users page role Select (apps/web/src/app/[orgSlug]/admin/
     users/page.tsx) picks ENGINEER. Existing canManageUsers guard; only
     ADMIN/OWNER may assign ENGINEER in this slice.
  2. Progress lead: the project's admin/staff page team member list
     (project-team-list.tsx expandable rows) gains a "Progress lead" toggle,
     ADMIN/OWNER-only, write path inherits the existing ADMIN-only ProjectTeam
     RLS wall.
- Display labels: `siteMemberLabel(member)` helper reads TeamMember.photoLead
  per project team: "Site Manager" when true, "Site Engineer" otherwise.
  Photo `createdBy` renders that label; per-project correct when a person
  leads on one site and engineers on another.
- Uploader names render on customer-facing photos (trust win: buyers see
  real people on their site).
- RLS: `progressphoto` policies grant SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE to ENGINEER scoped
  by ProjectTeam; UPDATE/DELETE of another member's photo additionally
  requires the actor's TeamMember.photoLead=true on a team linked to the
  photo's project.

## Recommended Approach
Approach C, sequenced into three slices so value lands early:

- Slice 1 (upload + staff preview): new backend module `customer-progress` (NestJS,
  `withRlsContext()` everywhere, matches module conventions). CRM side: photo
  upload per unit (staff phone flow, under 30 seconds), phase label per upload.
  Uploaders: site manager and site engineers (ENGINEER role +
  TeamMember.photoLead, per the Site-staff model above). ENGINEER role value
  ships in slice 1's migration (see T2 below) since the first uploader may be
  an engineer on day one.
  `ProgressPhoto.phaseId` references the existing `model Phase` in the Prisma
  schema (per-project phase records staff already manage); no new taxonomy is
  introduced. The timeline page in this slice is staff- and admin-facing only
  (preview inside the existing CRM auth); no customer authentication exists yet.
  Customer go-live, customer logins and push activation all land in Slice 2,
  where the Customer role and session are built.
- Slice 2 (portal shell + auth + customer live): CUSTOMER role added in this
  slice's part of the single role migration, customer login, portal routes
  outside `[orgSlug]` under a dedicated route group, and the customer-facing
  timeline page reading the same customer-progress data scoped to the
  customer's Booking via RLS. Web push on new upload activates here
  (PushSubscription/PushNotification models exist); push is best-effort, with
  the timeline page as the source of truth when a customer has no subscription
  granted. The shell absorbs the timeline built in Slice 1. Role migration
  note: ENGINEER (slice 1) and CUSTOMER (slice 2) each ship as their own
  complete four-touchpoint migration — never piecemeal.
- Slice 3 (read-only portal features): booking details, unit/villa summary with
  the unit number as the primary identifier (each cell links to its own entity),
  payment view, documents view. Messages/chat deferred; the Message models exist
  if the client wants it later.
- Slice 4 (supervision polish): photo correction/delete UI (supervisor-only
  via photoLead), invite/remove engineer flows completing T4's action set.

Data model additions: a `ProgressPhoto` model (unitId, phaseId, storage key,
caption, takenAt, createdBy) rather than overloading Activity. Photos live in
object storage behind signed read URLs scoped by RLS, not in the database.

Distribution: existing pipeline covers this. Deployed the same way as the current
three images (web Vercel, backend + realtime-sse on the VPS via Coolify); push to
main auto-deploys. No new distribution channel.

## Open Questions
- How often do buyers actually email for progress? (Sets push frequency and SLA
  expectations; does not change the architecture.)
- Which object storage is already in use for CRM file uploads? Slice 1 must match
  the existing pattern; confirm before implementing.
- Does the client want customer-facing messages/chat in v1 or v2? (Deferred by
  default; Message model is ready either way.)

## Design Specification (from /plan-design-review, 2026-10-26 — all decisions approved)

### Information hierarchy
```
STAFF: unit page (CRM, org-scoped, existing) gains a "Progress" tab
  1st: camera/upload button (primary, thumb-reach on phone)
  2nd: photo strip, newest first, tiny phase chips
  3rd: "View as customer" preview link

CUSTOMER: /portal home = the timeline itself (no dashboard, no menu)
  1st: unit identity ("Villa 14, Green Meadows") + current phase name
  2nd: photo timeline, newest first, month separators
  3rd: bottom tab bar: Progress | Details | (slice 3: Payments, Docs)

Navigation flow:
  /login (shared, role-routed) → customer → /portal (timeline)
  /login → staff → existing CRM
Desktop-for-customers: out of scope (premises target phones).
```
Constraint worship: on the customer home there is no search, no filter, no
settings — one unit, zero choice.

### Interaction states

```
---------------------|------------------|--------------------------|----------------------|--------------------|------------------

(slice 2)            | exact list       | soon" + unit identity +  | retry; never blank   | phase labels       | more button
                     | shape from       | "we'll notify you when   | internet errors      |                    |
                     | lead-timeline    | the first photo lands"   |                      |                    |
Staff upload         | camera opens     | unit w/o photos: big     | toast w/ reason,     | photo appears in   | slow network:
(slice 1)            | instantly        | CTA "Add first photo"    | photo queued local,  | strip + toast      | upload continues
                     |                  |                          | retry auto           |                    | in background
Session expired      | —                | —                        | redirect /login w/   | —                  | —
(customer)           |                  |                          | "session expired"    |                    |

(lead.email blank)   |                  | "photos not linked yet,  |                      |                    |
                     |                  | contact office" + phone  |                      |                    |

                     | navy, no spinner |                          |                      |                    |
```

### Journey storyboard
```
STEP | USER DOES                    | USER FEELS              | PLAN SPECIFIES?
-----|------------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------
1    | Gets login invite via        | Curious, slightly       | Invite message w/ portal
     | WhatsApp after booking       | skeptical               | name + one-line promise:
     |                              |                         | "See your home as it's built"
2    | First login, no photos yet   | Mild disappointment     | Empty state (Pass 2) +
     |                              |                         | "notify me" promise
3    | First photo lands, push      | THE moment: it's real.  | Push tap → deep-link to
     |                              |                         | timeline, photo full-bleed
4    | Monthly check (5-min habit)  | Anticipation ritual     | Month separators, newest first
5    | Construction slows/monsoon   | Anxiety, then relief    | Phase chip on every photo
6    | Handover                     | Gratitude, closure      | "Ready to move in" phase chip
```
One authored motion moment: photo entrance on upload-push deep-link,
ease-out; prefers-reduced-motion honored.

### Anti-slop constraint
Portal screens: timeline rows (list, not cards), phase chip + date + photo
per row; no decorative cards anywhere; the photo is the visual anchor; one
accent (green) used only for the current phase chip and CTA.

### Design system mapping (brand.css is the source of truth; no DESIGN.md)
- Phase chip: existing status-badge soft variants (AA-override set);
  green for current phase only, neutral for past phases
- Photo surface: `--background`; borders `--border` (not `--input`)
- CTA: `--primary` navy with `--primary-foreground`
- Typography: Inter, body ≥16px, no display type (OPERATE surface)
- Components: @paalstack/react-ui Props API only (Button, Card, Skeleton,
  toast — as lead-timeline.tsx already imports)

### Responsive & accessibility
- Viewports: phone 360-430px primary; tablet 768px secondary; desktop
  1024px+ centers timeline at max-w-lg
- Touch targets ≥44px; photo tap = lightbox, swipe to dismiss
- Keyboard: top-to-bottom focus; bottom tab bar is a `<nav>` with links
- ARIA: `<nav aria-label="Portal">`; timeline is `<ol>`; phase chip
  aria-label carries the full phase name; photo alt = caption or
  "Construction photo, {phase}, {date}"; new-photo badge via
  aria-live=polite; contrast inherits the brand.css AA compliance set

### Pass-7 decisions
- D4: Multi-unit customer → unit switcher chip on home timeline
- D5: Push tap after cold start → deep-link straight to the photo,
  full-bleed
- D6: Caption convention → "{phase} {month}, {unit}" auto-filled, staff
  may edit

## Success Criteria
- A customer can log in, see their unit, and see the latest construction photos
  within one login. Measured: first login to last photo in under a minute on a
  mid-range Android phone.
- A site engineer or site manager can upload a photo to a unit in under 30
  seconds from their phone, and the customer's timeline updates without a page
  rebuild.
- No customer route ever exposes another customer's unit: RLS test in the
  database-first test pattern passes for customer-vs-customer isolation.
- An ENGINEER user cannot read any lead, chat, booking, or revenue surface:
  RLS test passes for engineer isolation.
- A non-photoLead engineer cannot correct or delete another member's photo;
  a photoLead member can: RLS + service tests pass.

## Dependencies
- New ENGINEER + CUSTOMER roles + RLS policies (database-first tests per repo
  convention; each role ships as one complete four-touchpoint migration).
- TeamMember.photoLead column + ProgressPhoto policies in the same batch.
- Object storage: RESOLVED — reuse the existing StorageProvider
  (local-disk dev, imagekit prod) per eng review D3 of the architecture
  findings; no signed-URL infrastructure.
- Web push plumbing is present in the schema but unverified for customer
  subscriptions; confirm when push activates in Slice 2.

## The Assignment

phone, per-unit, phase label from the existing Phase records), the ProgressPhoto

## What I noticed about how you think
- You said "Currently nothing happens, we have build a new system with all
  possible useful scenerios." That is a green-field answer: no workaround to
  respect, no migration. It freed the design to pick the smallest feed first.
- You chose the full portal (C) against the narrower recommendation. Your call,
  and the design honors it by sequencing: C as the destination, A as the first
  slice. You take the bigger bet but still get the wedge's speed.
- You asked "mobile apps or web apps" before anything else. Platform-first
  thinkers get architecture right and scope wrong sometimes; the premise doc
  exists to hold that line: web app, staff keep the native app.

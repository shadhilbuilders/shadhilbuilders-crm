# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

**Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Status**: proposed | accepted | deprecated | superseded by ADR-NNNN
**Deciders**: who was involved

## Context

What makes this a decision at all: the forces at play, the constraint that rules out the obvious answer, and what went wrong (or would go wrong) without it. Keep it under ten lines.

## Decision

The change, in one to three sentences, present tense. Start with what the code actually does now.

## Alternatives Considered

Delete this section when there was no genuine alternative, rather than inventing one.

### Alternative name

- **Pros**: what it bought.
- **Cons**: what it cost.
- **Why not**: the specific reason it lost.

## Consequences

Delete any subheading that would be empty.

### Positive

- What becomes easier.

### Negative

- What becomes harder or has to be maintained twice.

### Risks

- What could go wrong later, and how the code already guards against it.
